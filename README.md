# IBM HR Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

The **IBM HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau project developed using the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance** dataset.

The objective of this project is to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and other HR metrics to help organizations make data-driven HR decisions. The dashboard provides actionable insights through interactive visualizations and KPI cards.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across the organization.
- Identify departments with the highest attrition.
- Understand workforce demographics.
- Compare attrition by gender and age group.
- Evaluate job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Analyze employee attrition by education field.
- Provide HR insights for better workforce planning.

# ❓ Business Questions Answered

### 1. What is the overall employee attrition rate?
**Answer:** The organization has an overall attrition rate of **16.1%**, with **237 employees** leaving out of **1,470 employees**.

---

### 2. Which department experiences the highest attrition?

**Answer:**

- Research & Development – **133 employees**
- Sales – **92 employees**
- Human Resources – **12 employees**

Research & Development experiences the highest employee attrition.

---

### 3. Which age group has the highest employee turnover?

**Answer:**

Employees aged **25–34 years** experience the highest attrition, indicating that early-career professionals are more likely to leave the organization.

---

### 4. Does attrition differ by gender?

**Answer:**

- Male Attrition – **150**
- Female Attrition – **87**

Male employees have a higher attrition count than female employees.

---

### 5. Which education field has the highest attrition?

**Answer:**

- Life Sciences – **89**
- Medical – **63**
- Marketing – **35**
- Technical Degree – **32**
- Other – **11**
- Human Resources – **7**

Employees from the **Life Sciences** education field have the highest attrition.

---

### 6. How does job satisfaction vary across job roles?

**Answer:**

The dashboard compares Job Satisfaction Ratings (1–4) across different job roles.

Key observations include:

- Sales Executives represent the largest workforce.
- Research Scientists and Laboratory Technicians have high employee counts.
- Job satisfaction varies significantly across job roles, helping HR identify roles requiring attention.

---

### 7. Which employee segments require HR attention?

**Answer:**

The dashboard identifies the following employee groups requiring attention:

- Employees aged **25–34**
- Research & Development department
- Sales department
- Male employees
- Employees from the Life Sciences education field

These segments contribute most to employee attrition and should be prioritized for retention strategies.
